Pastor TIM & Paulina Yu

Tim Yu serves as the head pastor and the English-Speaking Congregation’s director here at God’s Family Church. He graduated from UCLA with a degree in psychobiology and from Fuller Theological Seminary, where he received his master’s degree in theology and a doctoral degree in clinical psychology. He is married to his wife, Paulina, with whom he has a daughter named Samantha. In addition to his love for ministry, he is passionate about basketball, movies, and photography.
